# Open Questions

Every unverified assumption and known implementation gap in this repo, consolidated in one
place. Numbers match `PLAN.md` §9 where that section already numbered an item, so
cross-references from code docstrings stay stable. Items 11-34 are new gaps discovered
during implementation, not present in `PLAN.md` §9. Items 4, 5, 10, 15, 20, 22 and 24 were all
answered by the `python -m data_universe.probe` run of 2026-11-03 (Massive host),
and v0.8.0's `first_reported_is_as_filed` finding confirmed the companyconcept basis
assumption; item 27's symbol-keying half is answered too (aggregates are keyed by the
symbol in use at the time).
